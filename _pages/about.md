I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at <a href='https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/people/yirong-wang/'>Northeastern University</a>. I study computer systems performance through queueing theory, stochastic modeling and simulation. My work spans workload characterization, trace-driven/discrete-event simulation, and the design of high-fidelity, generalizable benchmarks.

I am currently working with [Prof. Peter Desnoyers](https://www.ccs.neu.edu/home/pjd/) in the [Systems Research Group](https://srg.khoury.northeastern.edu/).
